# Brand Voice

## Core Identity

Couture Airbrush represents custom art, wearable art,
live art experiences, and original visual culture.

The brand should feel artistic, established, authentic,
creative, and connected to real-world culture.

## Voice

The brand voice should be:

- Confident
- Creative
- Professional
- Authentic
- Culturally aware
- Artist-led
- Not overly corporate
- Not generic

## What Makes the Brand Different

The brand is built around original artwork and decades of
hands-on artistic experience.

The work includes wearable art, custom airbrush work,
live event art, commercial projects, and collaborations.

## Important Brand Rule

Marketing should never make the artist sound like a generic
print-on-demand seller.

Products should be presented as extensions of original artwork
and creative history.

## Content Style

Content should focus on:

- The artwork
- The story behind the artwork
- The artist
- The creative process
- Cultural relevance
- Wearable art
- Limited or unique pieces
- Live art experiences
- Past projects and accomplishments

## Avoid

- Generic sales language
- Fake urgency
- Invented accomplishments
- Excessive hype
- Copying other artists
- Making every post sound like an advertisement
